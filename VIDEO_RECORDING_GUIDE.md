# Demo Video Recording Guide

## 🎬 Requirements
- **Length**: Under 3 minutes (STRICT)
- **Platform**: YouTube, Vimeo, Facebook Video, or Youku
- **Visibility**: Public or Unlisted
- **Content**: Show actual functionality on the device

## 🛠️ Recording Tools

### Windows Options:
1. **OBS Studio** (Free, Professional)
   - Download: https://obsproject.com/
   - Best for high-quality recordings
   
2. **Windows Game Bar** (Built-in)
   - Press `Win + G` to open
   - Quick and easy

3. **ShareX** (Free)
   - Download: https://getsharex.com/
   - Good for quick recordings

### Recommended: OBS Studio
- High quality
- Easy editing
- Free and open source

## 🎥 Recording Tips

### Before Recording:
1. **Clean up your desktop** - close unnecessary windows
2. **Prepare test data** - have workspaces, tasks, and users ready
3. **Test your audio** - use a good microphone
4. **Practice the script** - time yourself
5. **Set up recording area** - 1920x1080 resolution recommended

### During Recording:
1. **Speak clearly and confidently**
2. **Move mouse deliberately** - not too fast
3. **Pause between sections** - easier to edit
4. **Show, don't just tell** - demonstrate features
5. **Keep energy up** - enthusiasm is contagious

### After Recording:
1. **Trim dead space** - keep it tight
2. **Add text overlays** (optional) - GitHub URL, key points
3. **Check audio levels** - clear and balanced
4. **Verify length** - MUST be under 3 minutes
5. **Export in high quality** - 1080p recommended

4. Description: Include GitHub repo URL
5. Visibility: Public or Unlisted
6. Tags: kiroween, hackathon, educollab, kiro, react, supabase

### Video URL Format:
- YouTube: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID`
- Vimeo: `https://vimeo.com/VIDEO_ID`
- Facebook: `https://www.facebook.com/watch/?v=VIDEO_ID`

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Video over 3 minutes (will not be watched beyond 3:00)
2. ❌ Poor audio quality (use a decent mic)
3. ❌ Not showing actual functionality (judges need to see it work)
4. ❌ Too much talking, not enough showing
5. ❌ Forgetting to mention Kiro usage
6. ❌ Private video (judges can't access)
7. ❌ Broken video link in submission
